## The interview comes before the first pixel

1. Audience: who sits in the room and what do they know
2. Occasion and time: the minutes set the slide budget
3. Goal: what the room should think, feel, or do after
4. Must-land: the one thing that cannot be lost

Plan goes to the user. Approved plans get built. Everything else goes back for edits.

## The build is assembly from a fixed system

Source material becomes an approved plan, the plan becomes slides, and the css, runtime, and motifs are pasted verbatim.

## Diagrams move to show the mechanism

A packet rides the wire, arrows draw in causal order, and the component doing the work pulses. Motion runs only on the active slide.
